# Speculation

**Difficulté :** —
**Type :** Divers, Divers
**But :** How to play Speculation a Victorian parlour game in which playing-cards are auctioned and traded - link to rules by David Parlett.

---

This was a popular 19th century parlour game in which players trade and auction cards, with the aim of acquiring the highest trump and thereby winning the pool. Further information and rules can be found on [David Parlett's Speculation page](https://www.parlettgames.uk/histocs/speculat.html).
