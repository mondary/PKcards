# Prefa

**Difficulté :** —
**Type :** Divers, Divers
**But :** Information on the Greek card game Prefa, a three-player trick-taking card game with bidding, closely related to Russian Preferans.

---

Prefa is the form of Preference played in Greece. As in all forms of this game, a 32-card pack is used and there are three active players. Greek Prefa resembles Russian Preference, rather than the versions played in other parts of Eastern Europe, in that the bidder can contract for more than 6 tricks.

[Rules of Prefa](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wAN6htD81B792Kzc67-H8pWn5fchBC-z/view) can be found in Eric Ball's [Card Game Rules](https://sites.google.com/site/filokritos/home/filokritos) collection.

Here is an archive copy of the Greek [Πρεφαδόρος](https://web.archive.org/web/20230205220613/https://sites.google.com/a/prefadoros.net/main/) website, which includes rules, strategy, a forum and the possibility to play Prefa online at [prefadoros.gr](http://www.prefadoros.gr/).

Thanos has published a free [Prefa program](https://thanoscardgames.jimdofree.com/download/plain-trick-taking/prefa/) for Windows.
