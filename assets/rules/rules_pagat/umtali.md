# Umtali

**Difficulté :** —
**Type :** Rami, Rami
**But :** How to play the card game Umtali - link to rules for this Rummy game from colonial Rhodesia.

---

This unusual rummy game is said to have been popular on train journeys in colonial Rhodesia. Umtali is the former name of a market town on the Salisbury-Beira railway line.

The rules can be found on this [Umtali page](https://web.archive.org/web/wjm.0catch.com:80/umtali.html).
