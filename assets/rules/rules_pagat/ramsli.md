# Ramsli

**Difficulté :** —
**Type :** Divers, Divers
**But :** A Hungarian trick-taking game with four-card hands related to the French game Rams. Link to rules.

---

A Hungarian game related to the French game **Rams**. The Croatian game Raub is also similar. 

A version of Ramsli played by the Donauschwaben - the German settlers on the banks of the Danube - in 18th and 19th century Hungary is described on Rick Heli's [Card Games of the Donauschwaben](https://rick-heli.site/gene/cards.html) page.
